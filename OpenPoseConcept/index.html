Body Controls

Selected: none

Drag a point to move it. Shift-drag (or right-drag) to move it with its children. Mousewheel while dragging moves the point in depth.

With a point selected: drag the ring to twist around its axis, drag the orange helper to aim it. Hold Shift (or use the right mouse button) to carry its children along.
